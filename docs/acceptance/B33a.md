# B33a acceptance: permission-aware bounded Lead CSV export

## Delivered contract

- `POST /api/v1/leads/export/csv` requires both `leads:read` and `leads:export`; the application transaction resolves and applies the ordinary Lead read visibility plus the independent owner-derived export visibility.
- Only requested active, readable, unmasked scalar Lead fields may become cells. Tags, addresses, owner and relationship fields cannot be exported; Customer and parent-Lead conditions are revalidated as current exact visible targets before they can narrow a query.
- The service probes at most 5,001 IDs, loads rows in 200-ID batches, builds a complete UTF-8 BOM CSV within read-only `REPEATABLE_READ`, and rejects documents above 5,000 rows, 50 fields, or 16 MiB before an HTTP body starts.
- `LeadCsvDocument` owns the shared non-queueing local admission lease until streaming completes, fails, or is cancelled. REST uses only fixed `leads.csv`, `no-store`, and `nosniff` headers.

## Verification evidence
